--- NAGARMOTHA ---

[Intro]
Nagarmotha is the Hindi name for Musta (Cyperus rotundus). It shares the identical profile as Musta, known for treating diarrhea, fever, and digestive toxicity (Ama).

[Taxonomical Classification]
Kingdom: Plantae | Order: Poales | Family: Cyperaceae | Genus: Cyperus | Species: C. rotundus

[Morphology]
Perennial sedge with dark green leaves, triangular stems, and highly aromatic underground tubers.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Grows abundantly as a weed in moist areas and agricultural fields globally.

[Historical Background]
Used in Ayurvedic formulations and traditionally processed into essential oils for perfumery due to its earthy, woody scent.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Alpha-cyperone, beta-selinene, cyperene, and various sesquiterpenes.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Carminative, stomachic, promotes digestion, manages obesity, and reduces joint inflammation.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Pungent, Bitter, Astringent | Virya: Cooling | Vipaka: Pungent | Dosha: Pacifies Kapha and Pitta.

[Methods of Consumption]
Rhizome powder (churna) with warm water, or incorporated into herbal jams and decoctions.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Safe in recommended doses. High doses may exacerbate dryness or Vata imbalances.
