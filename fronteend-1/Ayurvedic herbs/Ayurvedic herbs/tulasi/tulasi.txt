1. Introduction

Tulsi, scientifically known as Ocimum tenuiflorum, is a sacred medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly called Holy Basil, Tulsi is valued for its ability to support respiratory health, strengthen immunity, reduce stress, and fight infections.

Category	Classification

Slightly hairy texture
Leaves
Small green or purple aromatic leaves
Oval-shaped with serrated edges
Flowers
Tiny purple or white flowers
Arranged in spike-like clusters
Plant Height
Usually grows 30–60 cm tall
4. Historical Background
Revered in India for thousands of years
Considered a sacred plant in Hindu tradition
Traditionally used for:
Treating cough and cold
Improving immunity
Reducing stress
Supporting respiratory health
5. Scientific Benefits
Respiratory Support

Helps relieve cough, cold, congestion, and supports lung health.

Stress Reduction

Acts as an adaptogen, helping the body manage stress and anxiety.

Immunity Booster

Supports immune function and helps fight infections.

Antibacterial and Antiviral Properties

Contains compounds that may help protect against microbes and infections.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and damage.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Primarily balances Vata dosha
Considered warming and rejuvenating
Used to support respiratory and mental wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Mild stomach irritation
Dizziness in sensitive individuals

People taking blood-thinning or diabetes medications should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
