1. Introduction

Kanchanar, scientifically known as Bauhinia variegata, is a medicinal tree widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly known as the Orchid Tree, it is valued for supporting lymphatic health, glandular function, and reducing Kapha accumulation in the body.

2. Taxonomical Classification
Category	Classification
Kingdom	Plantae
Family	Fabaceae
Genus	Bauhinia
Species	Bauhinia variegata
3. Morphology
Stem and Bark
Medium-sized deciduous tree
Gray-brown bark with rough texture
Leaves
Distinctive bilobed leaves shaped like a camel’s foot
Bright green in color
Flowers
Large pink, purple, or white orchid-like flowers
Fragrant and bloom in clusters
Fruits
Flat elongated seed pods containing multiple seeds
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Reducing glandular swelling
Supporting thyroid and lymphatic health
Managing cysts and nodules
Clearing excess Kapha
5. Scientific Benefits
Lymphatic Support

Traditionally used to support lymphatic drainage and detoxification.

Thyroid and Glandular Health

May help support healthy glandular function and reduce swelling.

Kapha Reduction

Helps reduce excess mucus and tissue accumulation associated with Kapha imbalance.

Anti-inflammatory Properties

Contains compounds that may reduce inflammation and support healing.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and damage.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Primarily balances Kapha dosha
Considered cleansing and detoxifying
Used for glandular and lymphatic wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Mild digestive discomfort
Dryness in some individuals

Pregnant women and people with chronic medical conditions should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
