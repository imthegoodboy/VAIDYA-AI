1. Introduction

Bilva, scientifically known as Aegle marmelos, is a sacred medicinal tree widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly known as Bael, it is valued for supporting digestive health, treating chronic diarrhoea, reducing intestinal inflammation, and balancing digestive fire without increasing Pitta.

Considered sacred in Indian tradition and associated with worship practices
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Chronic diarrhoea and dysentery
Digestive weakness
Intestinal inflammation
Balancing digestive fire (Agni)
5. Scientific Benefits
Digestive Support

Helps improve digestion and supports healthy bowel function.

Diarrhoea Management

Traditionally used to manage chronic diarrhoea and dysentery.

Intestinal Healing

May help reduce inflammation and irritation in the intestines.

Digestive Fire Balance

Supports digestive fire without excessively increasing body heat.

Antioxidant Properties

Contains antioxidants that help protect body cells from damage.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective

Considered grounding and digestive-supporting
Used to strengthen the digestive system and reduce intestinal disorders

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Constipation
Stomach heaviness
Mild digestive discomfort

People with severe constipation should use medicinal preparations carefully.
