1. Introduction

Amalaki, scientifically known as Emblica officinalis (also called Phyllanthus emblica), is commonly known as Amla or Indian Gooseberry. It is an important medicinal plant in Ayurveda and is valued for its high Vitamin C content, rejuvenating properties, and health benefits for digestion, skin, and hair.

5. Scientific Benefits
Rich Source of Vitamin C

Contains high levels of natural Vitamin C and antioxidants.

Digestive Health

Helps improve digestion and may reduce acidity and constipation.

Immunity Support

Strengthens immune function and helps protect against infections.

Skin and Hair Health

Supports healthy skin, reduces aging effects, and promotes hair growth.

Antioxidant Properties

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and free radical damage.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances all three doshas (Tridosha) — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
Considered a rejuvenating herb (Rasayana)
Used to nourish body tissues and improve vitality

Excessive consumption may cause:

Mild stomach discomfort
Increased acidity in sensitive individuals
