1. Introduction

Bhringraj, scientifically known as Eclipta alba (also called Eclipta prostrata), is a medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly known as the “King of Hair”, Bhringraj is valued for promoting hair growth, supporting liver health, and calming the nervous system.

2. Taxonomical Classification
Category	Classification
Kingdom	Plantae
Family	Asteraceae
Genus	Eclipta
Species	Eclipta alba / Eclipta prostrata
3. Morphology
Stem
Small branched herb with soft green stems
Grows close to the ground
Leaves
Narrow, elongated green leaves
Slightly rough texture
Flowers
Small white flower heads
Bloom singly on stems
Habitat
Commonly grows in moist and tropical regions
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally known for hair and liver care
Commonly used for:
Preventing premature greying
Promoting hair regrowth
Supporting liver function
Calming the mind and improving sleep
5. Scientific Benefits
Hair Health

May promote hair growth and help reduce hair fall and premature greying.

Liver Support

Traditionally used to improve liver function and detoxification.

Nervous System Support

Helps calm the mind and may reduce stress and mental fatigue.

Anti-inflammatory Properties

Contains compounds that may reduce inflammation and support healing.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and damage.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Vata and Pitta doshas
Considered cooling and rejuvenating
Used for hair nourishment and liver wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Mild stomach discomfort
Allergic reactions in sensitive individuals

People with chronic medical conditions should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
