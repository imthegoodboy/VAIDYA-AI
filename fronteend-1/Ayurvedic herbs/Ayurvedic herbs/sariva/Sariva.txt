--- SARIVA ---

[Intro]
Sariva (Indian Sarsaparilla) is a sweet, cooling root that effectively purifies the blood and soothes inflammatory skin conditions.

[Taxonomical Classification]
Kingdom: Plantae | Order: Gentianales | Family: Apocynaceae | Genus: Hemidesmus | Species: H. indicus

[Morphology]
A slender, twining semi-shrub with woody, aromatic roots that smell reminiscent of vanilla or root beer.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Found throughout Central, Southern, and Eastern India.

[Historical Background]
Traditionally used to make cooling summer drinks to prevent heatstroke and to clear the body of toxins.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Coumarino-lignoids, hemidesmine, essential oils, and saponins.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Treats eczema, psoriasis, and acne; acts as a diuretic; reduces burning sensations in the urinary tract; promotes a clear complexion.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Sweet, Bitter | Virya: Cooling | Vipaka: Sweet | Dosha: Excellent for pacifying Pitta and Vata.

[Methods of Consumption]
Root powder taken with warm water or milk, or as a cold-water infusion (Phanta) during summer months.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Very safe for long-term use. No known severe side effects when taken in recommended doses.
