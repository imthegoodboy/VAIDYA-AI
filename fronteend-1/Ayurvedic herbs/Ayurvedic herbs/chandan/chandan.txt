1. Introduction

Chandan, scientifically known as Santalum album, is a fragrant medicinal tree widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly known as Sandalwood, it is valued for its cooling, calming, and skin-soothing properties. Chandan is traditionally used to reduce burning sensations, support urinary health, calm the mind, and soothe inflamed skin.

5. Scientific Benefits
Cooling and Soothing Effects

Helps reduce body heat and burning sensations.

Skin Health

Traditionally used for acne, inflammation, rashes, and irritated skin.

Urinary Support

May help soothe urinary discomfort and support urinary tract health.

Mental Calmness

Its aroma promotes relaxation and may reduce stress and anxiety.

Antimicrobial Properties

Contains compounds that may help fight microbes and infections.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective

Considered cooling, calming, and purifying
Used for skin care, emotional balance, and heat-related conditions

Excessive use may cause:

Allergic reactions in rare cases
