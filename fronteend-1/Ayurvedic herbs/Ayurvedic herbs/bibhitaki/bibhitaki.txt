1. Introduction

Bibhitaki, scientifically known as Terminalia bellirica, is a medicinal plant widely used in Ayurveda. It is especially valued for reducing Kapha dosha, supporting respiratory health, detoxifying body tissues, and improving voice quality. Bibhitaki is also one of the three fruits used in the Ayurvedic formulation Triphala.

5. Scientific Benefits
Respiratory Support

Helps clear excess mucus and supports healthy breathing.

Kapha Reduction

Traditionally used to balance excess Kapha in the body.

Eye Health

May support healthy vision due to antioxidant properties.

Detoxification

Helps cleanse body tissues and remove toxins.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and damage.

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Dryness
Stomach discomfort
Loose stools in some individuals

Pregnant women and individuals with chronic digestive problems should consult a healthcare professional before regular use.
