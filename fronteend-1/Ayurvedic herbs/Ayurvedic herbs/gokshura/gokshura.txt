1. Introduction

Gokshura, scientifically known as Tribulus terrestris, is a medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. It is valued for supporting urinary and reproductive health, strengthening the urinary tract, reducing inflammation, and improving vitality and stamina.

5. Scientific Benefits
Urinary Tract Support

Helps support healthy urinary function and urinary tract strength.

Kidney Stone Support

Traditionally used to help manage kidney stones and urinary discomfort.

Reproductive Health

May support reproductive wellness and healthy testosterone levels.

Prostate Support

May help reduce inflammation associated with prostate disorders.

Energy and Vitality

Traditionally used to improve stamina and physical performance.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Vata and Pitta doshas
Considered cooling and strengthening
Used for urinary, reproductive, and muscular wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Mild stomach discomfort
Restlessness in sensitive individuals

People with hormone-sensitive conditions or chronic illnesses should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
