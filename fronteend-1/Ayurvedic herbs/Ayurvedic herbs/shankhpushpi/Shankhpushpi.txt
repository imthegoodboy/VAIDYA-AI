--- SHANKHPUSHPI ---

[Intro]
Shankhpushpi is an esteemed 'Medhya Rasayana' (brain tonic) that enhances memory, sharpens intellect, and calms the nervous system.

[Taxonomical Classification]
Kingdom: Plantae | Order: Solanales | Family: Convolvulaceae | Genus: Convolvulus | Species: C. pluricaulis

[Morphology]
A small, trailing perennial herb with elliptic leaves and distinct, conch-shell shaped white or pale pink flowers.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Found growing wildly across the plains of India and North Africa.

[Historical Background]
Classical Ayurvedic texts describe it as the single most effective herb for boosting cognitive power and treating mental fatigue.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Convolvuline, shankhpushpine, phytosterols, and flavonoids.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Improves learning and memory, reduces stress and anxiety, helps manage ADHD in children, and supports deep sleep.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Bitter, Pungent, Sweet | Virya: Cooling | Vipaka: Sweet | Dosha: Balances all three doshas (Tridoshic).

[Methods of Consumption]
Whole plant powder taken with milk, ghee, or warm water. Also available as syrups.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Highly safe for all ages, including children. May cause mild drop in blood pressure in highly susceptible individuals.
