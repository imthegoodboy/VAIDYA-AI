1. Introduction

Dashamula is a classical Ayurvedic herbal formulation widely used in Ayurveda. The word Dashamula means “ten roots”, referring to a combination of ten medicinal plant roots traditionally used to balance Vata dosha, relieve pain, support recovery, and restore strength and vitality.

2. Components of Dashamula

Dashamula consists of two groups of roots:

Brihat Panchamula (Major Roots)
Bilva (Aegle marmelos)
Agnimantha (Clerodendrum phlomidis)
Shyonaka (Oroxylum indicum)
Patala (Stereospermum suaveolens)
Gambhari (Gmelina arborea)
Laghu Panchamula (Minor Roots)
Shalaparni (Desmodium gangeticum)
Prishnaparni (Uraria picta)
Brihati (Solanum indicum)
Kantakari (Solanum xanthocarpum)
Gokshura (Tribulus terrestris)

Traditionally used for:
Vata disorders
Neurological pain
Post-partum recovery
Fatigue and weakness
5. Scientific Benefits
Vata Pacification

Helps calm aggravated Vata and supports nervous system balance.

Pain Relief

Traditionally used for joint pain, muscular pain, and neurological discomfort.

Post-partum Recovery

Supports strength, tissue recovery, and energy restoration after childbirth.

Anti-inflammatory Properties

May help reduce inflammation and swelling.

Energy and Vitality Support

Helps improve stamina and reduce weakness and fatigue.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Primarily balances Vata dosha
Considered strengthening and rejuvenating
Used for musculoskeletal and nervous system wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Mild digestive discomfort
Loose stools in some individuals

Pregnant women and people with chronic illnesses should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
