1. Introduction

Chitraka, scientifically known as Plumbago zeylanica, is a medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. It is especially valued for stimulating digestion, improving metabolism, clearing excess Kapha, and supporting healthy weight management.

2. Taxonomical Classification
Category	Classification
Kingdom	Plantae
Family	Plumbaginaceae
Genus	Plumbago
Species	Plumbago zeylanica
3. Morphology
Root
Thick, branched medicinal root
Light brown outer surface
Stem
Semi-woody shrub with slender branches
Spreading growth habit
Leaves
Simple oval-shaped green leaves
Smooth surface with pointed tips
Flowers
Small white flowers arranged in clusters
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Improving digestion
Relieving constipation
Enhancing metabolism
Reducing excess Kapha and toxins
5. Scientific Benefits
Digestive Support

Stimulates digestive fire (Agni) and improves appetite and digestion.

Constipation Relief

Helps support bowel movements and digestive cleansing.

Metabolism Enhancement

Traditionally used to improve metabolic activity and energy utilization.

Kapha Reduction

Helps reduce excess mucus and heaviness associated with Kapha imbalance.

Weight Management Support

May support healthy weight management when combined with proper diet and lifestyle.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Kapha and Vata doshas
Considered warming and stimulating
Used to improve digestion and reduce toxins (Ama)

6

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Burning sensation
Acidity
Stomach irritation

Pregnant women and individuals with ulcers or severe gastric problems should avoid medicinal use without professional guidance.
