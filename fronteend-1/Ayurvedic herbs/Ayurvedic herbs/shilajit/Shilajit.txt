--- SHILAJIT ---

[Intro]
While not a plant, Shilajit is a vital Ayurvedic mineral pitch. It is a profound rejuvenator that restores energy and acts as a catalyst for other herbs.

[Taxonomical Classification]
N/A (Mineral Exudate/Humic substance)

[Morphology]
A sticky, black, tar-like resin that oozes from the rocks of high mountain ranges during the heat of summer.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Found primarily in the high altitudes of the Himalayas, Altai, Caucasus, and Andes mountains.

[Historical Background]
Known as the 'Destroyer of Weakness' in Sanskrit. Legends say it was discovered by observing white monkeys eating the pitch and becoming exceptionally strong.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Rich in fulvic acid, humic acid, and over 84 trace minerals in ionic form.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Boosts mitochondrial energy production (ATP), increases testosterone and male fertility, combats chronic fatigue, and improves brain function.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Pungent, Bitter, Salty | Virya: Heating | Vipaka: Pungent | Dosha: Balances Kapha and Vata.

[Methods of Consumption]
A pea-sized amount dissolved in warm water, milk, or green tea. Purified Shilajit is essential.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Must NOT be consumed raw/unpurified (contains heavy metals/fungi). People with high iron levels (hemochromatosis) or severe Pitta aggravation should avoid it.
