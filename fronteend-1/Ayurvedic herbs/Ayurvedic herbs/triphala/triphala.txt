1. Introduction

Triphala is a traditional herbal formulation used in Ayurveda. The word “Triphala” means “three fruits” and it is made from:

Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica)
Bibhitaki (Terminalia bellirica)
Haritaki (Terminalia chebula)

It is widely known for supporting digestion, detoxification, and overall body balance.

3. Morphology
Amalaki
Small round greenish-yellow fruit
Sour taste
Rich in Vitamin C
Bibhitaki
Oval-shaped gray-brown fruit
Hard outer covering
Haritaki
Green to yellow-brown fruit
Ribbed surface with elongated shape
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for thousands of years
Mentioned in classical Ayurvedic texts
Traditionally used for:
Digestive cleansing
Detoxification
Improving bowel movements
Promoting longevity and immunity
5. Scientific Benefits
Digestive Health

Supports healthy digestion and improves bowel regularity.

Detoxification

Helps remove toxins from the digestive tract and body.

Antioxidant Properties

Rich in natural antioxidants that protect cells from damage.

Immune Support

Helps strengthen immunity and maintain overall wellness.

Gut Health

May support healthy intestinal bacteria and reduce bloating.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances all three doshas (Tridosha) — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
Considered a gentle detoxifying and rejuvenating formula
Commonly used to improve digestive fire (Agni)

6

8. Precautions

Excessive intake may cause:

Loose stools
Abdominal discomfort

Pregnant women and individuals with digestive disorders should consult a healthcare professional before regular use.
