1. Introduction

Guduchi, scientifically known as Tinospora cordifolia, is a medicinal climbing shrub widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly called Giloy or Amrita, it is valued for its immunity-boosting, detoxifying, and rejuvenating properties.

2. Taxonomical Classification
Category	Classification
Kingdom	Plantae
Family	Menispermaceae
Genus	Tinospora
Species	Tinospora cordifolia
3. Morphology
Stem
Long climbing stem with green fleshy appearance
Contains small wart-like structures on the surface
Leaves
Heart-shaped green leaves
Smooth texture with long stalks
Flowers
Small yellowish-green flowers
Grow in clusters
Fruits
Small red or orange berries
Oval-shaped when mature
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Known as “Amrita”, meaning “nectar of immortality”
Traditionally used for:
Treating chronic fever
Strengthening immunity
Detoxifying the body
Improving vitality and recovery
5. Scientific Benefits
Immunomodulatory Properties

Helps regulate and strengthen the immune system.

Fever Reduction

Traditionally used to manage chronic and recurrent fevers.

Liver Support

Supports liver detoxification and healthy liver function.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects body cells from oxidative stress and damage.

Anti-inflammatory Effects

May help reduce inflammation and improve overall wellness.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances all three doshas (Tridosha) — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
Considered a rejuvenating herb (Rasayana)
Used to improve immunity and vitality

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Stomach discomfort
Constipation in some individuals

People with autoimmune disorders or diabetes should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
