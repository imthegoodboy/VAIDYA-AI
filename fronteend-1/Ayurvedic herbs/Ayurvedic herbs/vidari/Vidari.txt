--- VIDARI ---

[Intro]
Vidari is a deeply nourishing, massive tuber that acts as an aphrodisiac, energy builder, and anti-aging tonic.

[Taxonomical Classification]
Kingdom: Plantae | Order: Fabales | Family: Fabaceae | Genus: Pueraria | Species: P. tuberosa

[Morphology]
A large, fast-growing climbing vine with massive, fleshy underground tubers that can weigh up to 35 kilograms.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Found throughout India, particularly in the lower Himalayas and central Indian forests.

[Historical Background]
Used as a rejuvenating food-medicine (Rasayana) to build muscle mass, vitality, and treat extreme emaciation.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Puerarin, daidzein, genistein, and rich carbohydrates/proteins.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Boosts stamina and muscle strength, supports male and female reproductive health, improves breast milk production, and protects cardiovascular health.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Sweet | Virya: Cooling | Vipaka: Sweet | Dosha: Pacifies Vata and Pitta; increases Kapha.

[Methods of Consumption]
Tuber powder consumed with milk and ghee to maximize its tissue-building (Brimhana) properties.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Its heavy, sweet nature can exacerbate obesity and lethargy in high-Kapha individuals. Safe for long-term use.
