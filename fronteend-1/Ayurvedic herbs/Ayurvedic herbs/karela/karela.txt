1. Introduction

Karela, scientifically known as Momordica charantia, is a medicinal and edible plant widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly known as Bitter Melon or Bitter Gourd, it is valued for regulating blood sugar, purifying blood, supporting skin health, and improving metabolism.

Category	Classification

3. Morphology

Leaves
Deeply lobed green leaves
Soft textured surface
Flowers
Small yellow flowers
Bloom singly on vines
Fruits
Elongated green fruits with rough bumpy surface
Turns yellow-orange when fully ripe
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda and traditional medicine for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Managing blood sugar
Purifying blood
Treating skin disorders
Supporting digestion and metabolism
5. Scientific Benefits
Blood Sugar Regulation

May help maintain healthy blood glucose levels and improve insulin sensitivity.

Blood Purification

Traditionally used to detoxify and cleanse the blood.

Skin Health

Used for acne, rashes, and other skin conditions due to its cleansing properties.

Digestive Support

Supports digestion and may improve metabolism.

Antioxidant Properties

Contains antioxidants that help protect body cells from oxidative stress.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Kapha and Pitta doshas
Considered cooling and detoxifying
Used to reduce excess heat and sugar imbalance in the body

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Low blood sugar
Stomach cramps
Diarrhea in some individuals

People taking diabetes medications and pregnant women should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
