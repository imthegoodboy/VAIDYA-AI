1. Introduction

Kamdudha Ras is a classical Ayurvedic mineral-herbal formulation widely used in Ayurveda. It is mainly known for balancing Pitta dosha, reducing acidity, cooling the body, and relieving burning sensations and digestive discomfort.

Unlike Pippali (Piper longum), Kamdudha Ras is a formulation, not a single herb.

2. Main Ingredients

Kamdudha Ras is traditionally prepared using ingredients such as:

Mukta Shukti Bhasma (Pearl Oyster Shell Ash)
Pravala Bhasma (Coral Calcium)
Shankha Bhasma (Conch Shell Ash)
Giloy Satva (Tinospora cordifolia)
Purified mineral compounds

Ingredients may vary slightly depending on the preparation method.

5. Scientific & Medicinal Benefits
Acidity Relief

Helps reduce acid reflux, hyperacidity, and stomach burning.

Cooling Effect

Provides a cooling effect to the body and helps reduce excess heat.

Digestive Support

Supports digestion and may reduce gastric irritation.

Ulcer Support

Traditionally used to soothe ulcers and inflammation in the digestive tract.

Pitta Balancing

Helps calm aggravated Pitta dosha symptoms such as irritation and burning sensation.
