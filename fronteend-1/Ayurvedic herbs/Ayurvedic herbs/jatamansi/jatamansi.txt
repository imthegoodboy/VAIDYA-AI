1. Introduction

Jatamansi, scientifically known as Nardostachys jatamansi, is a medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. Native to the Himalayan region, it is valued for its calming effects on the nervous system and is traditionally used to reduce anxiety, improve sleep, and support emotional balance.

4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda and traditional Himalayan medicine for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Reducing anxiety and stress
Treating insomnia
Supporting mental calmness
Promoting emotional stability
5. Scientific Benefits
Anxiety and Stress Reduction

Helps calm the nervous system and may reduce anxiety symptoms.

Sleep Support

Promotes relaxation and supports restful sleep.

Emotional Balance

Traditionally used to improve mood and mental well-being.

Neuroprotective Properties

Contains antioxidants that may help protect brain cells from damage.

Anti-inflammatory Activity

May help reduce inflammation and support overall wellness.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Vata and Pitta doshas
Considered calming and grounding
Used to support mental and emotional health

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Drowsiness
Mild stomach discomfort
Dizziness in sensitive individuals

Pregnant women and people taking sedative medications should consult a healthcare professional before regular use.
