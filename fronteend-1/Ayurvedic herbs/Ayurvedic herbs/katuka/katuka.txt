1. Introduction

Katuka, scientifically known as Picrorhiza kurroa, is a medicinal herb widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly called Katuki, it is valued for supporting liver health, stimulating bile production, reducing inflammation, and helping detoxify the liver.

2. Taxonomical Classification
Category	Classification
Kingdom	Plantae

Species	Picrorhiza kurroa
3. Morphology
Root and Rhizome
Thin elongated rhizomes and roots
Brown outer surface with bitter taste
Main medicinally used part
Leaves
Long narrow green leaves
Arranged in rosette form near the base
Flowers
Small pale blue or white flowers
Bloom in spike-like clusters
Habitat
Commonly found in Himalayan alpine regions
4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Jaundice and liver disorders
Digestive and bile-related problems
Fever and inflammation
Detoxification and cleansing
5. Scientific Benefits
Liver Support

Helps support healthy liver function and detoxification.

Bile Stimulation

May stimulate bile production and improve fat digestion.

Anti-inflammatory Properties

Contains compounds that may reduce inflammation and tissue irritation.

Jaundice Management

Traditionally used in managing jaundice and hepatic disorders.

Antioxidant Activity

Protects liver cells and body tissues from oxidative stress.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances Pitta and Kapha doshas
Considered cooling and detoxifying
Used for liver cleansing and digestive wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Diarrhea
Stomach irritation
Excessive cooling effect in some individuals

Pregnant women and people with severe digestive weakness should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
