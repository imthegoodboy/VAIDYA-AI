1. Introduction

Ashwagandha, scientifically known as Withania somnifera, is a medicinal herb belonging to the Solanaceae (nightshade) family. It is one of the most important herbs in Ayurveda and is commonly called Indian Ginseng or Winter Cherry. It is well known for reducing stress, improving stamina, and supporting overall health.

4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for more than 3000 years
Mentioned in ancient Ayurvedic texts like:
Charaka Samhita
Sushruta Samhita
Traditionally used for:
Increasing strength and stamina
Reducing stress and fatigue
Improving sleep and memory
Promoting longevity
5. Scientific Benefits
Stress and Anxiety Reduction

Acts as an adaptogen, helping the body cope with stress and lowering cortisol levels.

Nervous System Support

May improve memory, concentration, and sleep quality.

Energy and Stamina

Helps increase physical endurance and reduce fatigue.

Immune Support

Contains antioxidant compounds that strengthen immunity.

Anti-inflammatory Properties

May help reduce inflammation and joint pain.

Hormonal Support

Supports thyroid function and reproductive health.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Primarily balances Vata dosha
Considered a rejuvenating herb (Rasayana)
Used to strengthen the body and calm the nervous system

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Drowsiness
Stomach upset
Diarrhea

Pregnant women and people with thyroid disorders should consult a doctor before regular use.
