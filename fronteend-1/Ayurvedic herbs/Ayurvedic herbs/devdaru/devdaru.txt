1. Introduction

Devdaru, scientifically known as Cedrus deodara, is a large evergreen tree widely used in Ayurveda. Commonly called Himalayan Cedar, it is valued for its anti-inflammatory, respiratory-supporting, and muscle-relaxing properties. The name “Devdaru” means “wood of the gods” in Sanskrit.

4. Historical Background
Used in Ayurveda for centuries
Traditionally used for:
Joint pain and inflammation
Fever management
Respiratory congestion
Muscle relaxation and pain relief
5. Scientific Benefits
Anti-inflammatory Properties

May help reduce joint inflammation and muscular pain.

Respiratory Support

Helps clear excess Kapha and congestion from the respiratory tract.

Fever Management

Traditionally used to support relief from chronic and intermittent fever.

Muscle Relaxation

Acts as a natural muscle relaxant and may reduce stiffness.

Antimicrobial Activity

Contains compounds that may help protect against infections.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective

Used for respiratory and musculoskeletal wellness

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Skin irritation
Stomach discomfort
Allergic reactions in sensitive individuals

Pregnant women and people with chronic medical conditions should consult a healthcare professional before regular medicinal use.
