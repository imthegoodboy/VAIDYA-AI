1. Introduction

Haritaki, scientifically known as Terminalia chebula, is a highly valued medicinal plant in Ayurveda. Often called the “King of Medicines”, Haritaki is known for supporting digestion, detoxification, sensory health, and longevity. It is also one of the three fruits used in the Ayurvedic formulation Triphala.

5. Scientific Benefits
Digestive Health

Supports healthy digestion and helps relieve constipation.

Colon Detoxification

Helps cleanse the digestive tract and remove toxins.

Antioxidant Properties

Contains antioxidants that protect body cells from oxidative stress.

Sensory Support

Traditionally believed to support the proper functioning of the five senses.

Anti-inflammatory Effects

May help reduce inflammation and improve overall wellness.

6. Ayurvedic Perspective
Balances all three doshas (Tridosha) — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha
Considered a rejuvenating herb (Rasayana)
Used to improve digestion, detoxification, and vitality

8. Precautions

Excessive use may cause:

Loose stools
Abdominal discomfort
Dehydration in some individuals

Pregnant women and people with severe weakness should consult a healthcare professional before regular use.
