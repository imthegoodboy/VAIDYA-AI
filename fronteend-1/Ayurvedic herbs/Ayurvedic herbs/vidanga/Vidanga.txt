--- VIDANGA ---

[Intro]
Vidanga is renowned in Ayurveda as the premier herb for destroying parasitic worms (krimighna) in the digestive tract.

[Taxonomical Classification]
Kingdom: Plantae | Order: Ericales | Family: Primulaceae | Genus: Embelia | Species: E. ribes

[Morphology]
A large climbing shrub with slender branches. The fruits are small, round, and turn red to black upon ripening, closely resembling black pepper.

[Habitat and Distribution]
Found in the hilly regions of India, Sri Lanka, and Southeast Asia.

[Historical Background]
Trusted since ancient times to protect against water-borne and food-borne parasites, especially in children.

[Phytochemical Constituents]
Embelin, quercitol, tannins, and volatile oils.

[Scientific and Medicinal Benefits]
Potent anthelmintic (anti-parasitic), improves digestion, treats flatulence, manages obesity, and acts as a mild laxative.

[Ayurvedic Perspective]
Rasa: Pungent, Astringent | Virya: Heating | Vipaka: Pungent | Dosha: Pacifies Kapha and Vata.

[Methods of Consumption]
Fruit powder taken with warm water or honey on an empty stomach to expel parasites.

[Side Effects and Precautions]
Embelin has temporary contraceptive effects. Men and women actively trying to conceive should avoid Vidanga. Safe for children in proper doses.
